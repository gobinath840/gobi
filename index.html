About

A structured thinker who prototypes with intent.

Gobinath is currently pursuing his B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at CMS College of Engineering, Anna University, holding a CGPA of 8.0. His work leans toward architecture before implementation — before writing code, he maps out module relationships, data flows, and deployment roadmaps, treating even a college project like a real engineering system.

He's spent time as a Web Development Intern at Emglitz Technologies, shipping responsive, cross-browser-tested interfaces in HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript — and debugging them until the layout held up on every screen. Outside internships, he builds things end-to-end for the sake of understanding them: a full turn-based Ludo engine and a real-time Snake game, both in pure Python, both built from loops and lists rather than a game framework.

He also treats Claude AI as a thinking partner — using it to pressure-test system designs and workflows rather than to skip the design step entirely. That combination — foundational CS thinking plus fluency with modern AI tooling — is what he brings to a team.
